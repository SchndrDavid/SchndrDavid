### What I do

I work as a **digitalization specialist** in an injection-moulding plant, where I turn SAP exports and machine data into dashboards that people on the shop floor actually look at. On the side I study **business informatics** and write small self-hosted apps for things I got tired of paying a subscription for.

Most of what's here follows one rule: **it should work on your own machine, with no account, no cloud, and no internet.** Usually that means a single HTML file you can open by double-clicking it.

**Currently:** master's thesis on how to measure whether a bot actually plays the card game *Wizard* well — across different player counts.

### Projects

| | |
|---|---|
| [**brainmap**](https://github.com/SchndrDavid/brainmap) | A mind-mapping canvas for people who study by connecting things. Markdown nodes, nested groups, and links between ideas that sit far apart. |
| [**flashcards**](https://github.com/SchndrDavid/flashcards) | Drop in a `.txt`, get a deck. Tracks what you don't know yet. No subscription, no account, no internet. |
| [**tester**](https://github.com/SchndrDavid/tester) | Multiple-choice tests from a plain text file — write a question, mark answers with `+` and `-`, done. Random rounds, partial scoring. |
| [**gymtrack**](https://github.com/SchndrDavid/gymtrack) | A year of training at a glance, plus a workout runner that knows a barbell from a plank. |
| [**Handover**](https://github.com/SchndrDavid/Handover) | Self-hosted file drop between your own devices. Upload on your phone, grab it on your laptop, no cloud in the middle. |
| [**EasyMeme**](https://github.com/SchndrDavid/easymeme) | Drop a photo in, type a caption, get a meme with a white bar and black text — then share it. |
